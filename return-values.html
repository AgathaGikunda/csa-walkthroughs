void or Not? What Comes Back from a Method

Every FRQ gives you helper methods to call. First learn to read a method header, then practice: decide what comes back, and how to call it the right way.

Three facts first

void means nothing comes back. The method does a job, like printing or changing a score, and that's it.
Any other word means a value comes back. int, double, boolean or String tells you its type.
Catch what comes back. Save it in a variable of the same type, or it is lost.

How to read a method header

Look at the word right before the method's name. That word tells you what comes back.

publicskip for now
intwhat comes back
scoreInRoundthe name
(int round)what goes in
publicskip for now
voidNOTHING comes back
printReportthe name
()nothing goes in

So how do I call it?

The word is void

Call it on its own line. There is nothing to save.

printReport();

The word is a type, like int

Save it in a variable of that same type.

int score = scoreInRound(2);
Think of a vending machine. A non-void method hands you something. If you don't hold out your hand (a variable), it falls on the floor and is gone.
